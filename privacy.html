Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

drop scheduler (“the app”) is a personal tool used only by its owner with the owner’s own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives from TikTok:

  • an access token and refresh token, which let the app upload videos to that account’s drafts;
  • the account’s open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected.

The app does not read followers, messages, likes, analytics or any other account data.

Videos

The app uploads only video files placed on the owner’s server by the owner, and sends them only to TikTok (scope video.upload).

Notifications

After each upload the app sends a short notice with the video’s caption to the owner’s own Telegram chat via the Telegram Bot API.

Storage and sharing

  • All data is stored only on the owner’s private server.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The app and this website use no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the app is removed from the server. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, in the list of connected third-party apps. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
