drop scheduler

A personal tool that uploads my own videos to my TikTok drafts on a schedule, so I can publish them from my phone.

How it works

  1. I put my finished videos into a folder on my private server.
  2. Twice a day the server takes the next video and uploads it to my TikTok account as a draft, using the official TikTok Content Posting API.
  3. TikTok sends me an inbox notification. I review the draft in the TikTok app, add the caption and publish it myself.

Who it is for

This is a non-commercial tool for my own account only. It is not offered to other users and it never publishes anything without my action in the TikTok app.

Permissions

  • user.info.basic: to confirm which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send my videos to my TikTok drafts.
